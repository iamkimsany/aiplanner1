# Product Brief — Just Start

## One Line
An AI-powered planner that reads your materials, knows your schedule, and tells you exactly what to do today — based on how much energy you have right now.

## The Origin
Built out of frustration with Notion: habits had to be written manually every day, and static task lists didn't adapt to real life. The goal was simple — a to-do list where habits reset automatically at midnight, tasks are generated by AI based on deadlines and available time, and everything is tracked forever without manual updates.

## The Problem
People don't lack goals. They lack a system that fits their real life.

Existing tools fail in three ways:
- **Notion / Todoist**: require manual setup every day, no energy adaptation
- **Habit trackers**: reset weekly, no connection to bigger goals
- **AI chat (ChatGPT)**: you have to ask it every time — it has no memory of your life

## The Solution
Just Start connects three things no other app combines:

1. **Your fixed schedule** — school, sleep, gym — so it only plans in free windows
2. **Your energy right now** — low / medium / high — so tasks match your state
3. **Your deadline + material** — reads your PDF, counts days left, divides content into sessions

It's not a chat wrapper. It remembers your context, resets habits automatically, and every morning tells you one thing: *here's what to do today, and here's when.*

## What Makes It Different

| | Notion | Todoist | AnySchedule | Just Start |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Auto-resets habits daily | no | no | no | **yes** |
| Adapts to energy level | no | no | no | **yes** |
| Reads your study material | no | no | no | **yes** |
| Counts days to deadline | no | partial | no | **yes** |
| Focus mode + app blocking | no | no | no | **yes** |
| Simplifies task if stuck | no | no | no | **yes** |

## Core Promise
> "Tell it what you want to achieve and by when. It figures out the when and how — around your life."

## Target Audience (v1)
Students aged 17–23 with chaotic schedules, multiple goals, and habits they keep resetting manually.

## MVP Success Metric
> User completes at least 1 task per day for 3 consecutive days.
